I'm a final-year Ph.D. candidate in [Dr. Jacob Durrant's lab](https://durrantlab.pitt.edu/) at the University of Pittsburgh, working at the intersection of computational structural biology and machine learning for drug discovery. My focus is building open-source tools and large-scale datasets for structure-based drug design.

Right now I'm developing LIGNOVA, an automated Python pipeline that docks bioactive PubChem compounds against experimentally determined protein structures to generate protein–ligand datasets for training machine-learning models — over 125 million poses across 175,200 compounds and 4,180 protein structures so far, backed by a competitive NSF ACCESS allocation to scale it nationally.

Alongside that, I've published as co-first author in *PNAS* (2025) and contributed to *MolModa*, a browser-based molecular docking tool published in *Nucleic Acids Research* (2024).

I'm now seeking industry roles in computational drug discovery, machine learning for chemistry, and cheminformatics / scientific software development. I'm open to relocation.
